About

The link between corporate projects and the people who make the materials.

Large corporate projects depend on hundreds of supply decisions. Muthu takes ownership of that relationship layer — finding the right manufacturers, agreeing fair terms, and keeping material flowing to site.

Working with IT companies and MNCs, Muthu represents the buyer's interest across steel, brick, cement and finishing supply — comparing options, checking quality and coordinating dispatch.
